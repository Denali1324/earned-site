Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Short version: your hours, pay and timesheets stay on your iPhone. We don't have accounts or a server that stores them, and we don't sell your data.

This policy explains what the Earned: Hours & Pay Tracker app ("Earned", "we", "us") does with information. Earned is made by Denali Enzmann, an independent developer in the United States.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into Earned is saved only on your device:

  • Your jobs, hourly rates, work days, pay rules and tax settings
  • Your shifts, breaks, notes and edits
  • People you've sent a timesheet to (their name and email)

We never receive this information. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or your computer, Apple's backup may include it, under Apple's own privacy terms. Deleting the app deletes this data from your phone.

What leaves your phone

Earned uses two outside services. Here is exactly what they get:

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
RevenueCat An anonymous ID created by the app, your subscription and purchase history from Apple, and basic device details (like iOS version and country). To check your subscription and unlock the app.
Meta (Facebook SDK) App events such as installing, opening the app, starting a trial and subscribing, plus basic device details. Your device's advertising ID only if you choose "Allow" when asked about tracking. To measure which of our ads lead to people using Earned.

Neither service receives your hours, pay, notes, timesheets or the people you send them to.

Tracking and your choice

The first time you open Earned, iOS asks whether to allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Earned doesn't share your advertising ID, and Meta only receives the limited app events Apple permits. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your card or payment details. Apple shares your purchase status with us (through RevenueCat) so the app knows you've subscribed.

Contacts

When you send a timesheet, you can pick someone from your contacts. Earned only sees the one contact you pick, and only uses their name and email to fill in who to send it to. It doesn't read or upload your contact list.

Notifications

Reminders (like "Still on the clock?" and "Break ends in 5 min") are scheduled on your phone. No reminder data is sent anywhere.

Sending your timesheet

When you email or share a timesheet, it goes through your own Mail app or the share sheet you choose. We don't receive a copy.

Children

Earned is meant for people 18 and over who earn an hourly wage. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your rights

Because your work data never reaches us, the simplest way to delete it is to delete the app. To ask about or delete the purchase records and app events described above, contact us and we'll take care of it with RevenueCat and Meta. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have extra rights to access, correct or delete personal information; contact us to use them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.

Contact
