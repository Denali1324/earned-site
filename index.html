Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Earned and tap Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

I got a new phone. How do I get my subscription back?

Open Earned and tap Restore purchase on the subscription screen, or in Settings inside the app.

Why is my take-home different from my paycheck?

Earned estimates taxes from your rate, hours, state and filing status. Your real paycheck can include other deductions. Open a job's Tax estimate > Advanced to add 401(k), insurance and extra withholding for a closer number.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
